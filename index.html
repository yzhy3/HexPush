How To Play

Push them off. Don't get pushed off. That's it.

Desktop

Mobile
Left thumb = joystick. Right thumb = Push / Dash / Guard.

Rules
• No health. Every clean hit knocks the enemy back exactly one hex tile.
• Guard blocks a push and staggers the attacker — but the meter runs dry.
• Get hit mid-swing and you stagger: free combo for them.
• Glowing tiles are about to collapse. The arena never stops shrinking.
• Fall off and you lose. Simple.
